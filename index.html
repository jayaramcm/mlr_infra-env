GOT QUESTIONS?

We've Got You Covered.

Clear technical details on site footprint, utilities, quality verification, and minimum order quantities.

We require an accessible, leveled laydown area of approximately 4,000 to 8,000 sq ft. This footprint accommodates the hydraulic casting machinery, raw aggregate stockpiles, and curing laydown beds. Firm soil or compacted ground is ideal.

For on-site machinery mobilization, we typically recommend a minimum order of 25,000 CC bricks or 500 running feet of precast fencing. For smaller quantities in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, supply can be dispatched directly from our Kamareddy mother plant.

Continuous water supply is needed for concrete batching and curing, along with a 3-phase commercial electrical hookup (40 to 60 HP load). If grid electrical power is unavailable on raw ground, we mobilize our own dedicated generator units.

Every batch complies with IS 2185 and IS 456 quality standards. We cast standard test cubes at regular intervals and provide certified compression test laboratory reports for site civil engineer sign-off prior to laying.

On-site manufacturing eliminates diesel long-haul freight surcharges, avoids 8% to 14% road transit breakage, and prevents idle labor downtime by synchronizing production directly with daily masonry speed.
